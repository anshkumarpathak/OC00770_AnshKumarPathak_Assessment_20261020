# Change and Transport Policy (sample)

## Transport path
Every change moves from the development system to the quality system and then to production. Each step is tested and signed off by the client.

## Production imports
Transports are imported to production only after Change Advisory Board approval, in the weekly window on Thursday at 18:00 IST. The support assistant never imports transports to production.

## Emergency changes
An emergency change needs a P1 ticket and written approval from the client IT head before it can be imported outside the weekly window.
