# SLA Escalation Procedure (sample)

## When to escalate
A ticket is breached when its resolution deadline has passed. A ticket is at risk when less than 25% of its resolution window is left, or less than 2 hours are left. Breached and at-risk tickets are escalated to the AMS delivery manager. Tickets that are on track are not escalated.

## What an escalation contains
An escalation lists each ticket with its client, priority, resolution deadline, time left and current owner, and proposes the next action, such as assigning an owner or adding a senior consultant.

## Approval of escalations
The AMS delivery manager approves every escalation before it is sent. The assistant never contacts the client directly; the delivery manager informs the client service manager about any breach.

## Protecting the SLA record
Ticket priority is never lowered and tickets are never closed to avoid a breach. Changing priority or status to hide a breach misreports the contractual SLA.
