# AMS Support SLA (sample)

## Priority definitions
P1 means a production system is down or a business-critical process such as invoicing or month-end close is stopped for many users. P2 means a critical process is badly affected but a workaround exists. P3 means a single user or a non-critical process is affected. P4 is a question, a how-to request or a minor change.

## Response times
Premium plan clients get a response within 15 minutes for P1, 1 hour for P2, 4 business hours for P3 and 1 business day for P4. Standard plan clients get a response within 30 minutes for P1, 2 hours for P2, 8 business hours for P3 and 2 business days for P4.

## Support hours
P1 incidents are handled 24x7. All other priorities are handled Monday to Saturday, 9:00 to 18:00 IST.

## Escalation to a consultant
A key user can ask for a human consultant at any time. The assistant raises a ticket, and a consultant calls back within 1 business hour for premium plan clients and within 4 business hours for standard plan clients.
