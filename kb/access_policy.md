# SAP Access and User Policy (sample)

## Password reset
SAP passwords are reset by the user through the self-service password portal. The support assistant and consultants never set, read or share passwords.

## Locked users
A user locked after too many failed logins can ask the support assistant to unlock their own SAP user. A user locked by an administrator can only be unlocked by the client security administrator, so the assistant raises a ticket instead.

## Role requests
New SAP roles are requested through the support desk. Every role assignment must be approved by the client authorisation owner before it is applied. Approved roles are applied within 1 business day.

## Sensitive roles
Sensitive roles such as SAP_ALL, SAP_NEW, Z_BASIS_ADMIN, Z_FI_PAYMENT_RUN and Z_DEBUG_CHANGE are never assigned through the support desk. They need a formal change request with a segregation of duties review.

## Role catalogue
Standard roles that can be requested are Z_FI_DISPLAY, Z_FI_AP_CLERK, Z_FI_CONTROLLER, Z_MM_DISPLAY, Z_MM_BUYER, Z_SD_STORE, Z_WM_OPERATOR and Z_REPORTING.
